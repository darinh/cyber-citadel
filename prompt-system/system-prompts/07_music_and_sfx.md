# 07 — Optional sourced music and restrained sound design

Audio supports attention and pacing; it must not compete with explanation or practice.

## Music

Music is off by default unless the user requested it or the delivery concept has a clear rationale.
When enabled, use only public-domain, CC0, CC-BY, or explicitly licensed recordings. Do not generate
music. Store the file locally, preserve author/title/source URL/license/attribution, and add it to the
credits.

Use instrumental beds, conservative narration ducking, and clean transitions. Listen to the final
mix on ordinary speakers and headphones. Silence is preferable during dense reasoning, data
interpretation, source demonstrations, and learner pause-and-do activities.

## Sound effects

Default to `sound_design: "minimal"`: transitions and feedback moments only. Use `none` when effects
would distract. `cinematic` is appropriate only for an explicitly approved narrative treatment.
Never add a sound on every slide or beat by habit.

Place approved assets in `assets/music/` or `assets/sfx/`, record provenance, and continue to
`08_script.md`.
